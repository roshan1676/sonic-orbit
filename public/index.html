About this prototype

Local Web Audio rendering and optional Demucs music separation. Browser HRTF plus two measured SADIE II profiles for AI objects; these are not calibrated to your ears. Dolby Atmos encoding and native surround decoding are not implemented. Multichannel input is downmixed before processing. Stereo-speaker mode uses tone processing or a conventional stereo AI remix.

Welcome to SonicOrbit.

Open a song or video, press Play, then switch between Original and Spatial. Start at a comfortable volume.

Head tracking: choose Camera, enable tracking, then face the screen and hold still. The first use downloads a small verified face model; camera frames stay on your PC. A face camera needs your face visible.

AI music tracks: use Open by path. This optional feature requires Python 3.11, FFmpeg and the local AI environment. In the Windows package, run Setup-AI.cmd once after installing those prerequisites. Ordinary playback works without AI.
